Plumbline Privacy Policy

Every post, true to your brand, before it goes live.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

Plumbline is a brand-governance and social media management web app built by Team Real Matrix as a student project. It helps teams check posts against their approved brand rules before publishing, and lets signed-in users connect social media accounts to publish posts and manage comments. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Plumbline" mean Team Real Matrix.

2. What we collect

DataDetails
Account dataYour name, email address, a hashed password (we never store your plain password), your role and workspace ID, and sign-in timestamps. Session tokens are stored only as hashes.
Workspace contentWhat you enter or upload: brand guidelines (tone rules, terminology, approved facts, colours, fonts), rulebook documents (PDF, Markdown or text) and their searchable extracts, post drafts, platform variants, audit findings, review comments, and approval history.
Connected social accountsWhen you connect an account, we receive from that platform: the account or Page ID, name and handle, the permissions you granted, and an access token (and refresh token where the platform provides one). Social contentPosts you publish through Plumbline, and comments on your connected accounts that we fetch so you can read and reply to them, including the commenter's public name or username and the comment text.
Usage and technical dataStandard server logs such as IP address, request time and errors, used to run and secure the service.

We do not collect your social media passwords. You sign in on each platform's own page and approve access there.

4. How we use data

  • To create and secure your account and workspace.
  • To check your drafts against your brand rules, explain findings, and compute a brand risk score.
  • To generate platform-specific suggestions and draft replies that you review. Nothing is published or sent without a person's action.
  • To publish posts and send replies to the platforms you connected, only when you choose to.
  • To show review history, team activity and governance insights inside your workspace.
  • To keep the service running, fix problems and prevent abuse.

5. AI processing and service providers

To provide AI features, the text of your drafts, comments and the relevant brand rules or rulebook passages is sent to AI providers through our server. We do not use your content to train our own models. The providers process data under their own terms, and we cannot guarantee their retention practices. Do not enter information into Plumbline that you are not allowed to send to these providers.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers above as needed to run Plumbline, with the social platforms you choose to publish to, with other members of your own workspace according to their role, or when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

7. Retention

We keep account and workspace data while your account exists. Social access tokens are kept until you disconnect the account or the token expires, and are deleted when you disconnect. Rulebook documents and drafts are kept until you delete them or your account is deleted. Server logs are kept for a short period and then rotated by our hosting providers.

8. Security

Traffic between your browser and Plumbline uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Social access tokens are kept on the server and are never sent to your browser. Access to the database and API keys is limited to our team through our hosting providers' controls. Because Plumbline is a test build, access tokens are not yet encrypted at the application level; we are working on additional protections. No system is perfectly secure, so we recommend disconnecting accounts you no longer need.

9. Your choices and data deletion

Disconnect a social account

Open Channels in Plumbline and use the disconnect button on the account. This deletes the stored access tokens from our database.

Revoke Plumbline's access on the platform

  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings, then Apps and Websites (Business Integrations), then remove Plumbline.
  • Threads and LinkedIn: remove Plumbline from the permitted apps or services in your account settings.
  • X: Settings, Security and account access, Apps and sessions, then revoke Plumbline.
  • Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions, then remove Plumbline.

Delete your Plumbline data

To delete your account and all associated data (account details, workspace content, rulebooks, drafts, connections and fetched comments), email YOUR_EMAIL_HERE from the address registered to your account with the subject "Delete my Plumbline data". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it at the same address.

If you removed Plumbline through Facebook or Instagram and want us to delete data we received from Meta, use the same email address and mention that you did so.
